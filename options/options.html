Lucid Reader

A reading view for PDFs. Most of the controls live in the reader itself — open any PDF and press S.

You're set up

  1. Open any PDF link in Chrome — it lands in Lucid Reader.
  2. Press S for the reading settings: fonts, spacing, colours, bionic, pacing.
  3. Switch modes at the top: Normal, Read-along, Auto-scroll, RSVP, or Original PDF for the untouched pages.
  4. For PDFs on your own computer, turn on Allow access to file URLs on this extension's details page in chrome://extensions.

You can also drag a PDF straight onto the reader window.
